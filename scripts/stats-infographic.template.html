How to read this. Telemetry is opt-in and off by default, so these are the installs that chose to share — a lower bound, not the whole user base. Country and model figures are stored as monthly aggregates with no link back to an install, so a model count is install–months claiming that model, not distinct users. No account, location or device identifiers are ever sent.
